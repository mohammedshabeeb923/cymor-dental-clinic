About Our Clinic

Why Choose CYMOR Super Speciality Clinic?

Located in the heart of Ponnani, near AV Higher Secondary School (Marva Building), CYMOR was established on 28th April 2002 by Dr. Nazeer P. Chittayil. What started as an ambitious dental facility has grown over two decades into an acknowledged Super Speciality Dental Centre.

Following 7 successful initial years of clinical practice, Dr. Nazeer completed post-graduate specialization in Orthodontics. By 2012, CYMOR cemented its identity as a premier centre for Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopaedics, subsequently opening 3 branch clinics across Kerala in Purangu, Naripparambu, and Mandalamkunnu.
